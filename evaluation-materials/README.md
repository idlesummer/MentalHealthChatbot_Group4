# Evaluation Materials for Pebbles Mental Health Chatbot

This folder contains all materials needed to conduct the user and therapist evaluation study for the Pebbles CBT chatbot.

## Contents

### 📋 Survey Instruments

1. **`therapist-survey.md`**
   - Comprehensive evaluation form for mental health professionals
   - Completed once per scenario (4 total: 1 preset + 3 self-selected)
   - Covers:
     - Intent-specific evaluation (I1-I8)
     - Overall therapeutic quality
     - Comparison to Raw GPT
     - Clinical safety assessment
     - Professional recommendations

2. **`user-survey.md`**
   - User experience evaluation form for general participants
   - Completed once per participant (covers all 3 systems)
   - Covers:
     - Overall experience and helpfulness
     - Human-like quality assessment
     - Comparison across Pebbles, Raw GPT, and [Aidora]
     - Intent-specific effectiveness (user-friendly version)
     - Trust, safety, and future use intentions

### 📖 Guidelines & Protocol

3. **`evaluation-guidelines.md`**
   - Complete study protocol
   - Participant recruitment criteria
   - Session administration procedures
   - Data collection and analysis plans
   - Ethical considerations
   - Troubleshooting guide
   - Includes preset scenario for therapists (Appendix A)

## Study Overview

### Research Questions

This evaluation compares **Pebbles** (intent-based CBT chatbot) against:
- **Raw GPT** (GPT with basic CBT instructions but no intent structure)
- **[Aidora]** (comparison chatbot - [SPECIFY])

Key questions:
1. Is Pebbles more therapeutically effective than Raw GPT?
2. Does Pebbles feel more human-like and natural?
3. Does the 8-step intent structure help or hinder the experience?
4. Do therapists consider Pebbles clinically safe and appropriate?
5. Would users choose Pebbles for mental health support?

### Participant Groups

- **Therapists (n=5-8):** Licensed mental health professionals evaluating clinical effectiveness
- **Users (n=15):** General population evaluating user experience

### What Makes This Evaluation Unique

✅ **Intent-specific analysis:** Evaluates all 8 CBT steps (I1-I8) separately
✅ **Professional validation:** Therapist assessment ensures clinical rigor
✅ **Multi-system comparison:** Not just Pebbles alone, but vs. alternatives
✅ **Human-likeness focus:** Dedicated questions on conversational quality
✅ **Real scenarios:** Participants use actual stressful situations

## How to Use These Materials

### For Researchers Running the Study:

1. **Pre-Study:**
   - Read `evaluation-guidelines.md` thoroughly
   - Obtain IRB approval (if required)
   - Recruit participants
   - Set up data collection system (spreadsheet or digital survey platform)
   - Create randomization schedule for user system order

2. **During Study:**
   - Follow protocols in `evaluation-guidelines.md`
   - Administer appropriate survey (`therapist-survey.md` or `user-survey.md`)
   - Ensure informed consent
   - Monitor for participant safety

3. **Post-Study:**
   - Collect and digitize all surveys
   - Follow analysis plan in guidelines
   - Report findings

### For Participants:

- You'll receive the appropriate survey during your session
- Complete all sections honestly
- No right or wrong answers - we want your genuine experience

## Data Analysis

After data collection, you should have:

### Quantitative Data
- Likert-scale ratings for all items
- Net Promoter Scores (NPS)
- Preference rankings
- Demographic information

### Qualitative Data
- Open-ended feedback on strengths/weaknesses
- Comparative analysis narratives
- Suggestions for improvement
- (Optional) Chat transcripts

### Key Metrics to Calculate

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Overall Preference for Pebbles | >60% |
| Human-likeness Rating | >4.0/5.0 |
| Therapeutic Helpfulness | >4.0/5.0 |
| Net Promoter Score | >20 |
